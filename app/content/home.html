A little about me

Outdoor portrait

I learn by making things, asking better questions and sharing what I discover along the way.

My path into design has also taken me into community leadership, mentoring and, more recently, building my own products. The thread through it all is curiosity about how people learn, work and find their way forward.

I’m still learning to start before I have every answer. Building gives me something to question and improve; conversations with other people often change what I do next. That’s part of why I make time for Tech Growth Circle and share more of my thinking online.
